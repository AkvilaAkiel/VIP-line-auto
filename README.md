📌 Project Overview
An interactive Telegram bot designed for MistChina to automate and organize the internal break and lunch schedule for the VIP Management team. 
The bot replaces manual coordination ("bazaar-style" chaos) with a structured, real-time queueing system.

🔴 The Problem
The VIP management team faced challenges in coordinating break times, leading to:
Overlapping absences of key personnel.
Disorganized communication and "noise" in work chats.
Lack of transparency regarding who is currently available and who is next in line.

✅ The Solution
I developed a Python-based bot that acts as a central coordinator for team availability. 
It ensures that only a specific number of people can be on break at once and provides a clear visual representation of the current queue.
